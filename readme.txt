Ventura Heat
------------
https://louisgorenfeld.itch.io/ventura-heat
------------
Produced during the 2025 DOStember game jam!
https://itch.io/jam/dosember-game-jam

Art, music, code, and design, 2025 Extent of the Jam
Pre-alpha build 

Goal: Get to each checkpoint within the time limit

Arrow keys accelerate, brake, and steer. 

A shifts up, and Z shifts down. When A and Z are depressed,
the clutch is pushed in. Release it to finalize your shift.

ESC quits.

Minimum tried: 486 DX2/66; yielded about 35 fps
Fluid: Pentium P200 (maybe lower clocked pentiums too?)
Required: OPL3 (OPL2 support soon):
	While the audio is written to be OPL2-compatible, it does not yet work 
	on an OPL2. Use an OPL3-equipped card only, or mute the volume.

Build isn't fully optimized yet. 

Status bar left to right:
< SPEDOMETER KM/h >      < RPMS >      < TIME REMAINING >


Tips:
	- Learning to use the gearshift well is crucial. Keep the RPMs in the
	  sweet spot!

	- Crashing and going off road really hurts. Often it's a better
	  idea to cruise at a lower speed.

	- Stopping distance is quite long at higher speeds. Shift down to
	  help stop rapidly

	- You can cut it close to rival cars, especially if you get your
	  swerve on.
